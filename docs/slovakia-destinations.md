BTS, KSC and TAT were resolved individually by Travelpayouts `places2` and checked against their airport operators. This only verifies the identifiers and airports; route availability depends on the origin and dates. Neither regional places nor parks are submitted as airport codes. The interface must not imply an airline serves a particular route without live provider results.
